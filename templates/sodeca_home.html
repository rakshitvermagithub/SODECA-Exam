    SODECA Exam 2025-26

    SODECA refers to a category of courses, specifically Social Outreach, Discipline & Extra Curricular Activities, which are part of the curriculum. It is designed and implemented to enhance students' holistic development by integrating social, disciplinary, and extra-curricular elements into their engineering education.
    This portal is for students to submit thier entries for the examination of this course for the term 2025-26.

    REGISTER & SUBMIT FORMS

    General Instructions

    • Date of activities/Certificates: From 1 Jan 2024 till date of SODECA Exam.
    • Certificates/proofs must be clearly scanned. Only pdf files are allowed.
    • Certificates/proofs must be authentic and should clearly mention names, date, signature of the authority etc.
    • The exam will be conducted in offline mode. Attendance will be given after verification from batch counsellor.
    • Students will bring separate softcopies (only pdf) of all the certificates. Hardcopies NOT required.
    • Rename certificate/proof pdf file as: universityroll_studentname_eventname.
    • Student has to fill the FEEDBACK FORM for current semester in their ERP portal.
    • If a student is not having a certificate, any other document where their name is mentioned can be used.
